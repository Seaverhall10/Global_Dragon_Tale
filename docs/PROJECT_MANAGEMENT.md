# Project management — Global Dragon Tale

## Vision
Comparative research project mapping global serpent and dragon symbolism: stories, tales, history, and motifs. Primary sources first; contested readings labeled; no invented bridges.

## How we work
- Entries in `research/entries/` using `templates/ENTRY.md`
- Motif and culture indexes under `maps/`
- Council seats draft; Boss seals cross-lane claims
- Ancient Texts / Looking Beyond receive **feeds**, they do not own this corpus

## Protocols
- Cite primary text / inscription / standard critical edition when possible
- Hypothesis lines must start with `HYPOTHESIS:` and stay separable from `EVIDENCE:`
- Web research allowed; paste citations + short quotes, not whole copyrighted books

## Guidelines
- Prefer culture-native names (long, nāga, nachash) alongside English glosses
- Separate *creature taxonomy* (serpent vs dragon vs chaos monster) as the sources do — do not force one English word

## Rules
- Privacy class `normal`
- No living-person dossiers
- No force-push; commit/push only when Seaver asks (initial scaffold push was requested 2026-09-22)
